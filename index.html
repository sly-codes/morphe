Morphe

One cloud of points, five mathematical forms. Scroll and watch each one turn into the next.

θ = i · 137.5°   y = 1 - 2i / n

Scroll to begin · click anywhere to pulse

Knot

A (2, 3) torus knot. The line circles the center twice while winding three times through the hole, and the points slowly drift along it.

r = 2 + cos 3φ   (r cos 2φ, r sin 2φ, sin 3φ)

Wave

A flat grid, lifted by two crossing sine waves and a ripple that spreads from the middle.

y = sin(3x + t) · cos(3z + t) + sin(9d - 2t)

04 / 05

Galaxy

Three spiral arms. Points close to the core orbit faster than the ones on the edge, so the spiral keeps winding.

θ = 2πk / 3 + 3.4r + t / (0.35 + r)

05 / 05

Bloom

Back to the sphere, but its radius now follows the angles. The surface folds into petals that keep turning.

r = 0.8 + 0.22 · sin 4θ · sin(3φ + t)
